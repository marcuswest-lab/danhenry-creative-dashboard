Dan Henry — Full Sales Funnel Breakdown

Scope: 138 paid-ads appts in the Booked Calls CSV = 127 already-happened + 11 upcoming (excluded). Outcomes matched from Ben's tracker (name) + Jacques' Slack reports (email/name). Reported = appts whose closer outcome is known; No Match = appt not matched to a Ben or Jacques outcome (the segment — country/revenue/intent — is known from the application; only show/close is missing, so it stays in its real segment row). Show% = shows ÷ reported; Offer% and Close% are of shows (No Match excluded from rates). Cash = cash collected; Revenue = total contract value (incl. remaining payment-plan balances); Avg Cash/Deal = cash ÷ closes. Every table below totals to the same 127 in-scope appts.

What “No Match” means: these are booked appts whose prospect/contact data (name, and for Jacques email) didn't match any row in Ben's closer tracker or Jacques' Slack call-outcome reports — so we have no show/offer/close outcome for them. They stay counted in Appts (segment is known from the application) but are excluded from Show%/Offer%/Close%.
